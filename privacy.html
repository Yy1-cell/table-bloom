Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Table & Bloom (饭局) helps people find meal buddies and pick a fair meeting restaurant. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address if you sign in with email or Google (name and email from your Google account). Guests are given an anonymous account that lives on their device and has no email.
  • Profile: nickname, avatar emoji, and your fixed dietary needs (such as allergies or vegetarian).
  • Dining activity: tables you host or join, the starting point and craving you enter, this-meal dietary notes, and optional post-meal check-ins and journal entries.
  • Location: only the place you search for or choose, or your device location if you tap "Use my location". We do not track you in the background.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show tables, find a fair middle point between friends, and suggest restaurants.
  • To sign you in and send sign-in emails.
  • To keep the service safe and within its free usage limits.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see what

  • At a table, the host and the people at the table can see your nickname, avatar, starting point and dietary notes for that meal, as described inside the app. At a private table, everyone at the table can see them.
  • Your post-meal check-ins and "who I'd eat with again" choices are private to you.

Services we use

  • Supabase — database and sign-in.
  • Netlify — website hosting.
  • Brevo — sending sign-in emails.
  • Google — "Sign in with Google" and Google Maps. When a Google map is shown, Google receives the usual requests a map needs and handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — free map data for the built-in map.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. To have your account and data deleted, email us at the address below and we will do it. Guest accounts that are not used again may be removed during clean-ups.

Children

Table & Bloom is meant for adults. Please do not use it if you are under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
